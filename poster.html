Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered simply and clearly.

What is social media poster design?

These are graphic posters created for platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp. Their goal is to promote a brand and attract attention.

Why do I need social media poster design?

A professional poster builds your brand identity, increases engagement, and strengthens customer trust.

Which poster size is best for design?

For Instagram posts, 1080x1080 px is best; for Instagram Stories, 1080x1920 px; and for Facebook posts, 1200x630 px works best.

In what format are posters delivered?

You receive the poster in PNG and JPG formats. If needed, a PDF or editable source file can also be provided.

How much does social media poster design cost?

The price depends on the type and quantity of the design. A single poster usually costs between Rs. 500 and Rs. 4,000. If you need multiple posters each month, choose a monthly package for a lower per-poster rate. Share your needs to receive a free quote.

How can I earn money online in Pakistan?

You can earn through freelancing, blogging, YouTube, and affiliate marketing. First, learn a skill, then create a profile on Fiverr or Upwork.

What do I need to start freelancing?

You need a laptop, fast internet, a skill, and a professional profile. A degree is not required, but a portfolio is essential.
